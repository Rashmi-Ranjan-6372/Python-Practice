28. COMPLEX EXPRESSION
Question:
Predict:

y = 2
z = 3
result = x + y * z ** 2 // 2 - 1

Example Output:
13

Explanation:

2 * 9 = 18
18 // 2 = 9
5 + 9 - 1 = 13
